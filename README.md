## Player Guide

### Getting Started

Open the portal at the address your teacher has given you. You will land on the main **Play** screen.

Before you can play, you need to tell the portal who you are:

1. **Select your school** from the dropdown list.
2. **Select your year group** (e.g. Year 7, Year 8…).
3. Choose whether you want to play **vs a Student** or **vs the Computer**.
4. Press **Find a Game** (for online play) or **Start** (for computer play).

You do not need an account or password. Just pick your school and year and you are ready to go.

---

### Playing a Game

#### Finding an opponent
When you press **Find a Game**, the portal searches for another student who is waiting to play. This usually takes a few seconds. Once a match is found, the game starts automatically.

#### During the game
- The **chessboard** is in the middle of the screen.
- Your **name, school and clock** appear on the left side.
- Your **opponent's** info and clock appear on the right side.
- **Click a piece** to select it — valid squares will highlight.
- **Click a highlighted square** to move there.
- The **move list** on the right keeps track of every move played.

#### Clocks
Each player has their own countdown clock. When it is your turn, your clock counts down. If your time runs out, you lose on time. Keep an eye on it!

#### Captured pieces
Pieces you have taken are shown below your name. Pieces your opponent has taken are shown below theirs.

#### Pawn promotion
If your pawn reaches the other end of the board, a panel will pop up asking you to choose a piece — Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight. Click the one you want.

#### Ending the game
A game can end by:
- **Checkmate** — one player's king has no escape.
- **Resignation** — press the **Resign** button if you want to give up.
- **Draw offer** — press the **Draw** button to offer a draw; your opponent can accept or decline.
- **Time out** — a player's clock reaches zero.
- **Abandonment** — if a player disconnects and does not return within 30 seconds, the other player wins.

After the game, a result screen appears. You can leave **optional feedback** about your experience by pressing the feedback button.

#### Disconnection
If you lose your internet connection mid-game, the portal waits up to **30 seconds** for you to reconnect. If you come back in time, the game continues. If not, the game is recorded as a forfeit.

> **Tip:** Make sure you have a stable connection before starting a game, especially during a competition.

### Playing vs the Computer

Select **vs Computer** on the start screen and choose a difficulty level:

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Beginner | Makes random moves — great for learning |
| Easy | Plays simple moves, few tactics |
| Medium | Plays sensibly and will punish basic mistakes |
| Hard | Plays strong chess with tactics and strategy |
| Expert | Very strong — a real challenge! |

Press **Start** to begin. The game works exactly like an online game except there is no opponent — the computer moves automatically on its turn.

### Competitions

Go to the **Competitions** page from the navigation bar at the top.

Here you can see all current and upcoming competitions:

- **Live Now** — competitions you can join right now.
- **Paused** — competitions that are temporarily stopped.
- **Upcoming** — competitions starting soon; a countdown shows how long until they open.
- **Completed** — past competitions.

Each competition card shows the name, a description, which schools can participate, any year-group restrictions, and how many games have been played.

To join a competition, go back to the **Play** page. If a competition is currently open, you will see it listed when you set up your game. Select it before pressing **Find a Game**.

> Only competitions that are currently **Live** and available to your school and year group will appear.

---

### Leaderboard

The **Leaderboard** page shows how schools are ranked across the portal.

- **Cross-School view** — rankings based on games played against *other* schools. This is the main competitive ranking.
- **In-House view** — shows activity within each school (practice games against your own schoolmates).

The table shows:
| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Rank | Position (🥇🥈🥉 for top 3) |
| School | School name and total games played |
| Points | Total score (win = 1 pt, draw = 0.5 pt) |
| Wins | Number of wins |
| Draws | Number of drawn games |
| Losses | Number of losses |
| Games | Total games played |

Scroll down to see individual competition boards with full game results.

---

### Chess Coach

The **Coach** page is an interactive learning tool.

- Work through **puzzles** to sharpen your tactics.
- Get **move suggestions** and explanations.
- Study chess patterns and ideas at your own pace.
- Difficulty ranges from beginner puzzles to advanced positions.

You do not need to be in a real game to use the coach — it is available any time.

---

### News

The **News** page shows the latest announcements, updates, and stories from the IES Chess Club. Check here for competition announcements, results, and chess tips posted by your admin or coach.

---

### Find a Player

The **Find a Player** page shows which schools are currently online and when they typically play.

- A **green dot** means that school has players online right now.
- The **schedule grid** shows which days and times each school usually plays.
- Use this to know the best time to find an opponent from a specific school.

---

### Customising Your Board

During a game, you can personalise how the board looks:

- **Piece style** — choose from 14 different piece designs (Modern, Alpha, Neon, Wooden, and more).
- **Board colours** — change the colour of the light and dark squares.

Look for the customisation options on the game screen. Your choices are saved for next time.

---

### Tips & Good to Know

- You **do not need an account**. Just select your school and year each time.
- You will be given a **player number** (e.g. Player 042) that identifies you during a session.
- Games played count towards your **school's leaderboard score**, so play your best!
- If no opponent is available, try the **Computer** mode or check the **Find a Player** page to see when other schools are online.
- The portal works best on a **laptop or desktop** in a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
- If something goes wrong during a game — a crash, a freeze, or an unexpected result — let your teacher or admin know so they can check the game integrity log.

## Admin Guide

### Logging In

Go to **admin.html** on your portal. You will see a login screen asking for your email address and password.

- If you are a **new admin**, your account must first be approved by an existing admin or the super admin.
- If you have forgotten your password, use the **Forgot Password** link to receive a reset email.
- After logging in, you will land on the **Admin Dashboard**.

> Admin accounts are separate from player accounts. Players never log in to the admin panel.

---

### Dashboard Overview

The dashboard shows a live snapshot of portal activity:

- **Active Players** — students currently on the portal.
- **Games Today** — total games started today.
- **Live Competitions** — number of competitions currently running.
- **Registered Schools** — total number of schools in the portal.

The **status indicator** in the sidebar footer shows whether the portal is connected to the database in real time.

Use the **sidebar** on the left to navigate between all sections. Use the **scope selector** at the top of the sidebar to switch between managing all schools (Global) or a single school (if your account is scoped to one school).

---

### Managing Schools

**Configuration → Schools**

Here you can add, edit, and remove schools.

For each school you can set:
- **School name** — as it appears in the portal.
- **Year groups / Classes** — which years are enabled for that school (e.g. Year 7 through Year 11).
- **Play schedule** — the days and times that school's players are expected to be online (shown on the Find a Player page).

> Changes to school names appear immediately across the entire portal including the leaderboard and game screens.

---

### Competitions (Admin)

**Main → Competitions**

This is where you create and manage competitions.

#### Creating a competition
Press **New Competition** and fill in:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Name | Competition title displayed to players |
| Description | Short summary shown on the Competitions page |
| Schools | Which schools can participate (all or specific) |
| Year restriction | Limit to specific year groups (optional) |
| Cross-school only | If on, only games between different schools count |
| Start / End date-time | When the competition window opens and closes |
| Timer status | Live, Paused, or Upcoming |

#### Managing a live competition
- Set status to **Paused** to temporarily stop players from joining.
- Set status to **Ended** when the competition is over.
- The competition card on the public Competitions page updates automatically.

#### Availability windows
You can set specific **days and time slots** when the competition is open for play. Outside those windows, players cannot join — useful for running supervised sessions.

---

### Leaderboard Adjustments

**Main → Dashboard (or Competitions panel)**

If scores need correcting (e.g. a technical issue affected a result), you can apply **manual score adjustments** per school:

- Add positive or negative point adjustments with a reason note.
- Adjustments are visible in the leaderboard alongside normal match results.
- All adjustments are logged in the Audit Log.

---

### News Articles

**Main → News**

Create announcements, results, and updates that appear on the public **News** page.

For each article:
- **Title** — headline of the article.
- **Author** — who wrote it.
- **Body** — the main text (line breaks are preserved).
- **Image URL** — optional image shown at the top of the article (must be a direct link to an image file).

Articles are listed newest first. To remove an article, use the delete button.

---

### Appearance & Theming

**Configuration → Appearance**

Control how the portal looks for players.

- **Global theme** — sets the default colours and piece style for all schools.
- **Per-school theme** — override the global theme for a specific school (players from that school see the custom theme).
- **Piece style** — choose from 14 available piece styles.
- **Board colours** — set the light and dark square colours.

Changes apply immediately when players refresh their page.

---

### Play Times & Scheduling

**Configuration → Play Times**

Set when the portal is **open for play** globally:

- Enable or disable play entirely with the **Play Enabled** toggle.
- Define allowed **days and time slots** for play.
- Outside configured times, players see a message that the portal is not currently available.

This is separate from competition availability — it controls access to the portal itself.

> Useful for ensuring play only happens during supervised school hours.

---

### Game Integrity

**Main → Game Integrity**

The portal automatically flags games that look suspicious — for example, very short games, unusual result patterns, or games that ended unexpectedly.

For each flagged game you can:
- **Review** the move list and timestamps.
- Mark it as **Cleared** (no issue found) or **Flagged** (confirmed problem).
- Add a verdict note explaining the decision.

Cleared and flagged games are removed from the review queue but remain in the log.

---

### Audit Log

**Main → Audit Log**

Every admin action is recorded here with a timestamp, the admin who made the change, and what was changed.

- Logs are kept for **20 days**.
- Useful for tracking who made changes if something goes wrong.
- Adjustments, competition changes, news posts, school edits, and admin account changes all appear here.

---

### Admin Users

**Configuration → Admin Users**

Manage who has admin access to the portal.

#### Adding a new admin
1. Press **Invite Admin**.
2. Enter the new admin's name, email address, and set their **school scope**:
   - **Global** — can manage all schools.
   - **Single school** — can only manage their assigned school.
3. The new admin registers using that email address. Their account will appear as **Pending** until approved.
4. Approve the account from this panel.

#### Revoking access
Find the admin in the list and press **Revoke**. They will immediately lose access on their next page load.

#### Password policy
Admins set their own password when registering. Passwords must meet the minimum length requirement. Encourage admins to use strong, unique passwords and to not share their login.

> The **super admin** account is pre-configured and always has full global access. Contact your system owner if super admin access is needed.
